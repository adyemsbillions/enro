# Enro ad: voice-over script

**Video:** `enro-ad.mp4`, 82 seconds. Timings are when each line should **start**; each line fits before the next scene.
**Voice:** warm, relaxed, conversational, like telling a friend about something useful. Not a hype or announcer voice.
A natural Nigerian accent is ideal. Smile on the "earn" lines. Leave the pauses; the music fills them.
**Say "Enro" as:** *EN-roh*.

## Recording notes

- **Format:** record WAV at 48 kHz in a quiet room, with about 1 second of silence before the first line.
- **Takes:** read each line 2–3 times; the best takes can be picked in editing.
- **Pace:** about 2.5 words per second. If a line runs long, it's fine to drop "So," or "Just like that."
- **"Now… it pays":** take your time here. The music drops out for this line, so the voice carries it alone.
- **Mixing:** lower the music by about 10 dB under the voice and bring it back up in the gaps (this is called "ducking"). The sound effects (taps and coin chimes) can stay at full volume.
